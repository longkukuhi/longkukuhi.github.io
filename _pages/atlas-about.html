About Zijun Long

I am an Assistant Professor at Hunan University. I study how machines use language and images to find objects, identify them and detect defects.

Research

In interactive image retrieval, I study how a search system can use each new description to refine its results. In robotic perception and industrial inspection, I work on recognising objects and locating defects using visual references.

I am particularly interested in reliable vision–language models and in adapting them with limited data or training. I have published 16 peer-reviewed papers as first, co-first or corresponding author, including work at ICML, ECCV, SIGIR and ICRA.
