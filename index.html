Hunan University

I am an Assistant Professor at the College of Electrical and Information Engineering, Hunan University, where I have worked since January 2025. I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Glasgow in March 2025.

My research develops efficient and reliable multimodal learning for information retrieval, robotic perception and industrial visual analysis. I am particularly interested in interactive text-to-image retrieval, reliable vision-language models, and training-free or parameter-efficient adaptation.

I have published 16 peer-reviewed papers as first, co-first or corresponding author, including work at ICML, ECCV, SIGIR and ICRA. Recent work includes RAD (ICML 2026) on training-free anomaly detection, ADaFuSE (SIGIR 2026) on adaptive fusion for interactive image retrieval, RoboEye (ICRA 2026) on robotic object identification, and real-time electrical impedance tomography in IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics (2026).
